Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information

📋Information We Collect

When you use AKANS Social Store, we may collect:

  • Account details — your name and email address (via Google sign-in)
  • WhatsApp number — if you provide one for order updates
  • Order details — the service purchased and the destination link or in-game UID you submit
  • Wallet & transaction history — your balance and Add Money records
  • Referral data — if you use Refer & Earn or the Partner Program
⚙️How We Use Your Information
  • To process, deliver and track your orders
  • To manage your wallet balance and transactions
  • To track referrals and Partner Program earnings
  • To contact you about your orders or account
  • To improve and secure our services
🔗Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Google Firebase — for secure sign-in and data storage
  • Payment gateways — to process Add Money / wallet top-ups
  • Telegram / WhatsApp — only if you message us directly; those platforms have their own privacy policies
🛡️Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including secure storage and encrypted connections, to protect your data. Access to account data is limited to authorized personnel only.

🙋Your Rights

You can request to view, correct, or delete your account data at any time by reaching out through Help & Support.

🍪Cookies & Local Storage

Our app may use local storage on your device to remember your session and app preferences (e.g. for the installable app experience). We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

🔞Age Restriction

Our services are not intended for children under 13. If you are under 18, please use our services only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

📝Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of our services after changes means you accept the updated policy.
